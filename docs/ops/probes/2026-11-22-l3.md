   package gives the count only, not which step of the shape those sites fail.

## The second run: why the 4 sites differ

- **Date**: 2026-11-22, the same PC and executable, with the game closed. **How**: the package of label
  0.1.0-lodestar.2, built from `feat/lodestar` at 4569730, whose finder also says why a site the pattern alone accepts
  is none of the shape's, and which statics only those sites load. The console's line again: "to read first", then
  "the report is clean".
- **SHA-256**: package `a510c9dd52bd3f923f60a3806bcfc6c7b79f0b6f3d73e15bc57b1339d3fa8e98`; report
  `30a7e6f4bf9b1d1b7f8f3d1bfbe7424467bdbcde777bf0d26d48c056e678039d`. Reviewed as the first; `lint --report` and the
  sweep find it clean.

What it settled:

1. **The two runs agree in everything they share**: the image, every count, all 217 statics with their class objects
   and witnesses, the getter, the classes and the table are the same, value for value.
2. **All 4 sites differ at the shape's second step, and in one way**: the test is a 64-bit test of another register
   than the one the first step loaded. None is a load or a test of another width, and none is another instruction.
   So what those sites load in front of the test is not what they test: it is not the accessor's own load, and the
   thing loaded is not a manager's slot.
3. **They gave the pattern 2 statics that are none**: the pattern's accepted sites reach 219 statics, the shape's
   217, and the 2 more are loaded only at those 4 sites (3 and 1). Each stands beside a class object that already has
   a static of its own among the shape's (with 43 and with 497 sites). So under the pattern two managers had two
   statics each; among the shape's sites every class object goes with exactly one static, 217 to 217.
4. **So the shape is right to leave the 4 sites out, and its rule is kept**: the test must be of the register just
   loaded. The pattern did not look at registers. This also corrects the first run's reading above: the 4 sites are
   not loads of a narrower value, as was thought likely, but loads of something else beside a test of the manager's
   pointer already held in another register. That 2 of the 219 statics had no readable instance in R1's run 3
   (WHY§208) fits either reading, and stays LIKELY the same two.
